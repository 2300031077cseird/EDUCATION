About Apex Academy

Built on Excellence, Driven by Results

For over 15 years, Apex Academy has been the benchmark of quality education, nurturing young intellects into nation-builders.

A Legacy of Academic Mastery

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Find quick answers about our batches, demo classes, fee options, and hostel facilities.

Which classes and streams do you teach at Apex Academy?
We offer foundation courses for Classes 6th to 8th, board coaching for Classes 9th & 10th, Intermediate 11th & 12th (MPC, BiPC, MEC, CEC), and competitive exam coaching for IIT-JEE, NEET UG, EAMCET, Olympiads & NTSE.
Are free demo classes available before taking admission?
Yes! We provide 2 days of free demo classroom sessions so students and parents can evaluate our teaching methodology, smart classroom technology, and faculty interactions.
Do you provide both online and offline classes?
Yes, we operate a hybrid model. Students can attend physical classes on campus or stream live HD classroom lectures with real-time digital chat doubt resolution via our mobile & web portal.
Is hostel and transport facility available for outstation students?
Yes, we have separate air-conditioned hostels for boys and girls with 24/7 security, nutritious meals, study wardens, and dedicated bus transportation routes covering the entire city.
How are weekly tests and performance analysis conducted?
Tests are held every weekend on our Computer Based Testing (CBT) portal. AI analytics generate instantaneous reports highlighting time spent per question, accuracy rate, and national rank standings.
Are scholarship options available for merit students?
We conduct the Apex National Talent Scholarship Exam (ANTSE). Meritorious students can earn up to 100% tuition fee waivers based on their performance.
